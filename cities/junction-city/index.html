Living in
Junction City

Highway 99, about fifteen miles north of Eugene. Here’s what it’s like, and what a buyer should weigh first.

What it’s like here

Junction City sits on Highway 99 fifteen miles north of Eugene, in the middle of grass-seed and row-crop country. Unlike most valley towns this size it has an employment base of its own in recreational-vehicle manufacturing, which means a real share of residents work where they live. The town grid is compact and walkable; everything outside it’s farm ground.

Getting around

Highway 99 runs through the middle, fifteen miles north of Eugene — about twenty minutes. Highway 36 heads west into the Coast Range from just north of town.

What the town has

Junction City School District, a community hospital, a public library, and the Scandinavian Festival every August. The recreational-vehicle manufacturing base means a meaningful share of residents work in town, not commuting, which is unusual for a valley town this size. Grass seed and row-crop farm ground surrounds it in every direction, and Eugene is twenty minutes south on Highway 99.

Who it suits

Suits somebody who wants a town with an economy of its own and easy access to farm ground, who doesn’t need to be in Eugene every day, and who is fine with Highway 99 instead of the freeway.

What a buyer should think about first

There are jobs here, which is unusual for a town this size in the valley and changes what the housing market does when Eugene cools.

The market

Lane County's median sale price ran about $464,000 for the three months ending June 2026, down 3.5% from a year earlier, with a median of 18 days on market (Redfin).

Questions

Asked and answered

Is Junction City a good place to buy a house?

It depends entirely on what you want from a place, which is why we run a tour before we run a search. What we can tell you is what Junction City is like to live in and who it tends to suit — both are above — and we would rather talk somebody out of the wrong town than sell them into it.

What do homes cost in Junction City?

County-wide the median ran about $464,000 for the three months ending June 2026 (Redfin). A single town inside the county can sit well above or below that, and one quarter of sales can move a small-town median several percent. Ask us for the closed comparables on the street you’re considering.

What schools serve Junction City?

Junction City School District. Attendance is set by district boundary, not by city line, so confirm the specific address before you commit — two houses on the same road can feed different schools.

Should I drive the commute before I buy in Junction City?

Yes, and at the hour you would drive it. A route that reads as twenty minutes on a map at ten in the morning is a different number at five in the afternoon, and it’s the single most common thing buyers underestimate about moving out of the metro core.

Do you work in Junction City, or only in Eugene?

All of Lane County. Twenty-one advisors, several of whom live and work outside the metro core specifically because small-town and rural property behaves differently — wells, septic, acreage, access and county land use are normal parts of the job out here.

Can I see what’s for sale in Junction City?

Yes, and also what isn’t. A meaningful share of what we transact never reaches the RMLS, because we keep a curated list of more than 700 accredited and cash investors and we hear about property before it lists. Call and ask what’s quietly available.
