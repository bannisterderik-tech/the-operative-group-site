The mission

Every family in Lane County.
Not just the easy ones.

Help every family in Lane County make wise real estate decisions, with fiduciary representation, transparent and timely communication, and superior negotiation.

We also run our own in-house real estate ad agency, Liftoff Agent, which is why marketing is a line item we can actually control rather than one we outsource and hope about.

What we do

Help investors, local residents and out-of-state relocation clients with entering and leaving their real estate journey. In plainer terms: liquidate, 1031 exchange, and upgrade or downgrade a portfolio. The downsizing division handles retirement, probate and senior living. The relocation division builds the plan for moving in or out of Oregon.

The method

Eight steps, every time.

LISTEN

We listen

Your situation is yours alone. The first meeting is a listening appointment — we learn the journey and the goal before anyone talks about price.

RESEARCH

We research

Network, market data, and our own deals. We have bought and sold in this county with our own money, so the research is not theoretical.

ANALYZE

We analyze

Multiple entry and exit strategies, due diligence data, live market performance, and valuation two ways — income and price per square foot.

PLAN

We plan

The average transaction runs well over 100 steps. You get the order of operations, the timeline, and the budget in writing before we start.

EXECUTE

We execute

Professional photography and video, virtual tours, social, paid search, email, syndication, circle prospecting, targeted mail, and open houses — run by our in-house ad agency, Liftoff Agent.

CONNECT

We connect

Contractors, stagers, attorneys, estate planners, financial advisors, landscapers, accountants, property managers. Plus 700+ curated cash buyers and investors.

COMMUNICATE

We communicate

Weekly update calls and a transaction tracker you can actually follow. When something breaks, you hear it from us first — text, email, or phone.

CLOSE

We close

Some deals do not close. A proactive approach means you find that out early, not at day 40, and it costs you less when it happens.
