Six directions

The same homepage, the same words, the same photographs — rendered six ways. Each one is drawn from a site that actually sells at the top of this business: the Didone-and-brass restraint of Aaron Kirman, the italic-serif editorial of The Agency, the heavy-sans confidence of SERHANT. Open each on your phone and tell me which one to build out across every page.

Where it stands

What changes, what doesn’t

Each option changes the typeface, the colour, the shape language and the density, and four of them put a photograph behind the whole first screen. None of them touch the words, the structure or anything that passed the review — so picking one is a look decision, not a rebuild.

How to judge them

  • Open each on your phone. That’s where most of the traffic will read it.
  • Look at the first screen, the eight path cards, and the four exits — those carry the site.
  • Ask whether it looks like a team that owns property, not a team that sells it.

Tell me the one you want, or the two you want combined, and I’ll roll it across every page.
