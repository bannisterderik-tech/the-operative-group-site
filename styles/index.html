How to judge them

Three things worth looking at

Most of the people who read this site will read it on a phone, standing up, in about forty seconds. That’s the test.

One

The first screen

Does it say who you are before anybody scrolls? Four of the six put a photograph behind the whole thing.

Two

The eight paths

Selling, buying, investing, probate, relocating, downsizing, joining the team, the tools. Somebody has to find their own door quickly.

Three

The four exits

That block is the argument for the whole business. It has to read as a comparison, not as decoration.

What a choice does and doesn't change

Each option changes the typeface, the colour, the shape language and the density, and four of them put a photograph behind the whole first screen. None of them touch the words, the structure, the photographs or anything else that’s already been checked — every one of these renders from the same source.

Two of them can be combined. If you want the Estate typography with the Broadcast palette, that’s a reasonable ask and it isn’t extra work.

What happens nextSend back the number, or the two numbers. Whichever it is gets rolled across every page, and then the site’s ready for the domain.
