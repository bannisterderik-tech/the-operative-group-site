Southern Oregon

The Rogue Valley: a drier climate, a longer growing season, and a wildfire-insurance market that behaves unlike the Willamette Valley.

We can place you hereWorked through the team and REAL Broker’s referral network of nearly 10,000 agents.

What is here

Counties. Jackson (seat: Medford) · Josephine (seat: Grants Pass)

Principal cities. Medford · Grants Pass · Ashland · Central Point · Talent · Phoenix · Eagle Point

What matters here

  • Medford is the Jackson County seat and the commercial centre of the Rogue Valley; Grants Pass is the Josephine County seat on the Rogue River
  • Ashland carries Southern Oregon University and the Oregon Shakespeare Festival, which shape both its rental market and its seasonality
  • Wildfire risk scoring materially affects which carriers will write policies here, and a buyer who can’t get a binder can’t close
  • Medford, Grants Pass and Central Point are all above the 25,000 HB 2001 threshold

The rules that follow you here

Oregon sets rent increases, middle housing and probate at the state level, so most of what governs a transaction in Southern Oregon is identical to what governs one in Eugene. That’s genuinely useful: the reference library on this site works for any Oregon address.

Which counties does Southern Oregon cover?

Jackson County, seat Medford; Josephine County, seat Grants Pass. County seat matters more than people expect — it’s where probate is filed and where county land-use decisions are made.

Does the Oregon rent cap apply in Southern Oregon?

Yes. ORS 90.323 is statewide and for 2026 the maximum increase is 9.5% for most long-term residential rentals, 6% for manufactured home facilities and marinas with more than 30 spaces. Properties less than 15 years old are exempt. A local rule can be stricter than the state floor but never looser.

What about probate on a property in Southern Oregon?

Oregon probate is state law, so the thresholds are identical everywhere: a small estate affidavit is available at $275,000 or less with no more than $200,000 in real property and $75,000 in personal property, and it can’t be filed until 30 days after the date of death. What changes by region is which circuit court you file in — Jackson County files in Medford, Josephine County files in Grants Pass.
