Lake Oswego, Oregon

Population about 40,732. Incorporated 1910. In Clackamas County, seat at Oregon City.

Short answer

Lake Oswego is an incorporated city in Clackamas and Multnomah and Washington counties, Oregon, with a population of about 40,732. It incorporated in 1910, making it the 13th largest of Oregon’s 241 cities. Sitting above 25,000 people, it falls under HB 2001 middle housing in its residential zones.

Where it sits and what that decides

Lake Oswego straddles a county line: parts of it fall in Clackamas and Multnomah and Washington County. That matters more than it sounds. The county a parcel sits in decides which assessor values it, which recorder holds the deed, and which circuit court hears a probate over it.

Inside Clackamas County, Lake Oswego is the 2nd largest of 16 incorporated cities. At about 40,732 people, Lake Oswego is large enough to have its own market instead of borrowing one from the nearest bigger city. Comparable sales inside the city limit are usually the right comparable sales.

Incorporated in 1910. Cities that formed in that window generally have a pre-war core and rings of later building around it, and the inspection findings change sharply between the two.

Getting your bearings

Lake Oswego sits at 45.4207, -122.6706, about 5.4 miles south-east of the county seat at Oregon City. Eugene, where this team is based, is 97 miles south. Mail here carries 2 postal codes: 97034, 97035.

The nearest incorporated cities are Milwaukie (2.3 miles north-east), Rivergrove (4.1 miles south-west), Durham (4.2 miles west) and Johnson City (4.6 miles east). Straight-line distances — the road takes longer, and in the Coast Range or the Cascades it can take much longer.

What Oregon law does in Lake Oswego

Above the 25,000-person line, so HB 2001 middle housing reaches its residential zones. The 9.5% rent cap applies here as everywhere in Oregon. An estate is filed in Clackamas County Circuit Court or Multnomah County Circuit Court or Washington County Circuit Court, at Oregon City or Portland or Hillsboro.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is Lake Oswego, Oregon in?

Clackamas County and Multnomah County and Washington County — it straddles the line. The county seat is Oregon City / Portland / Hillsboro, which is where recording, assessment and probate happen.

How big is Lake Oswego, Oregon?

About 40,732 people, which makes it the 13th largest of Oregon's 241 incorporated cities and the 2nd largest of the 16 in Clackamas County. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does HB 2001 middle housing apply in Lake Oswego?

Yes. Lake Oswego is above the 25,000-person threshold, so its residential zones must allow duplexes, triplexes, quadplexes, cottage clusters and townhouses.

Where is probate filed for a Lake Oswego property?

In the Clackamas County Circuit Court at Oregon City or the Multnomah County Circuit Court at Portland or the Washington County Circuit Court at Hillsboro. Venue follows the county, and your attorney confirms it before filing.
