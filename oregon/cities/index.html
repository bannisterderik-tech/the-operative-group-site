Reference

Every incorporated city in Oregon

All 241 of them, with county, population and year of incorporation. Oregon runs land use, rent and probate at the state level, so most of what governs a sale is the same in all of them.

Short answer

Oregon has 241 incorporated cities across its 36 counties. The largest is Portland at about 652,503; the smallest is Greenhorn at 3. 24 of them are above the 25,000-person threshold where HB 2001 middle housing applies in full.

Oregon city data, retrieved September 2026

Checked September 2026

The oldest is Oregon City, incorporated in 1844. The newest is La Pine, which incorporated in 2006. Between them sit 239 others, and the statutes that matter — the rent cap, the middle-housing rules, probate procedure — reach all of them equally.

What changes from city to city is zoning, utilities, and which county recorder holds the deed. That is what these pages carry.

Questions

Asked and answered

How many cities are in Oregon?

241 incorporated cities, spread across 36 counties. Marion County holds the most; several counties hold only one or two.

What is the largest city in Oregon?

Portland, at about 652,503 people. It spans three counties — Multnomah, Washington and Clackamas.

What is the smallest incorporated city in Oregon?

Greenhorn, with a recorded population of 3. It is still a legally incorporated city with its own limits.
