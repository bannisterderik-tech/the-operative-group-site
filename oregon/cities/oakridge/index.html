Oakridge, Oregon

Population about 3,206. Incorporated 1935. In Lane County, seat at Eugene.

Short answer

Oakridge is an incorporated city in Lane County, Oregon, with a population of about 3,206. It incorporated in 1935, making it the 97th largest of Oregon’s 241 cities. It sits below the 25,000-person HB 2001 middle-housing threshold, so local zoning governs what can be built on a residential lot.

Where it sits and what that decides

Oakridge sits in Lane County, whose seat is Eugene. County business — recording, assessment, probate — happens there, not here.

Inside Lane County, Oakridge is the 8th largest of 12 incorporated cities. At around 3,206 people, Oakridge is small enough that a single unusual sale moves the apparent median. Anybody pricing a house here should be reading individual sales, not averages.

Incorporated in 1935. Much of the original stock in a city of this vintage is post-war, which usually means flat lots, straightforward foundations, and panels and roofs reaching end of life in neighbourhood-wide clusters.

What Oregon law does in Oakridge

Under the 25,000-person line, so HB 2001 middle housing doesn’t reach it and city zoning decides. The 9.5% rent cap applies here as everywhere in Oregon. An estate is filed in Lane County Circuit Court, at Eugene.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is Oakridge, Oregon in?

Lane County. The county seat is Eugene, which is where recording, assessment and probate happen.

How big is Oakridge, Oregon?

About 3,206 people, which makes it the 97th largest of Oregon's 241 incorporated cities and the 8th largest of the 12 in Lane County. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does HB 2001 middle housing apply in Oakridge?

No. Oakridge is below the 25,000-person threshold, so the full HB 2001 requirement doesn't reach it and the city's own zoning governs.

Where is probate filed for a Oakridge property?

In the Lane County Circuit Court at Eugene. Venue follows the county, and your attorney confirms it before filing.
