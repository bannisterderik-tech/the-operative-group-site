Salem, Oregon

Population about 175,535. Incorporated 1857. County seat of Marion County.

Short answer

Salem is an incorporated city in Marion and Polk counties, Oregon, with a population of about 175,535. It incorporated in 1857 and is the county seat, making it the 3rd largest of Oregon’s 241 cities. Sitting above 25,000 people, it falls under HB 2001 middle housing in its residential zones.

Where it sits and what that decides

Salem straddles a county line: parts of it fall in Marion and Polk County. That matters more than it sounds. The county a parcel sits in decides which assessor values it, which recorder holds the deed, and which circuit court hears a probate over it.

Inside Marion County, Salem is the 1st largest of 20 incorporated cities. Salem is one of a handful of Oregon cities past six figures. At that size there are enough sales every month that a price built from the last ninety days holds up, and a mispriced house is corrected by the market inside a fortnight.

It has been incorporated since 1857, which puts it among the oldest cities in the state — 5th oldest of the 241. Housing stock that old brings its own inspection list: original sewer laterals, unbolted foundations, knob-and-tube that somebody has partly replaced.

Getting your bearings

Salem sits at 44.9429, -123.0351 — and it’s the county seat. Eugene, where this team is based, is 62 miles south. Mail here carries 14 postal codes: 97301, 97302, 97303, 97304, 97305, 97306, 97308, 97309, 97310, 97311, 97312, 97313, 97314, 97317.

The nearest incorporated cities are Keizer (3.3 miles north), Turner (8.0 miles south-east), Independence (9.8 miles south-west) and Aumsville (11 miles south-east). Straight-line distances — the road takes longer, and in the Coast Range or the Cascades it can take much longer.

What Oregon law does in Salem

Above the 25,000-person line, so HB 2001 middle housing reaches its residential zones. The 9.5% rent cap applies here as everywhere in Oregon. An estate is filed in Marion County Circuit Court or Polk County Circuit Court, at Salem or Dallas.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is Salem, Oregon in?

Marion County and Polk County — it straddles the line. The county seat is Salem / Dallas, which is where recording, assessment and probate happen.

How big is Salem, Oregon?

About 175,535 people, which makes it the 3rd largest of Oregon's 241 incorporated cities and the 1st largest of the 20 in Marion County. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does HB 2001 middle housing apply in Salem?

Yes. Salem is above the 25,000-person threshold, so its residential zones must allow duplexes, triplexes, quadplexes, cottage clusters and townhouses.

Where is probate filed for a Salem property?

In the Marion County Circuit Court at Salem or the Polk County Circuit Court at Dallas. Venue follows the county, and your attorney confirms it before filing.
