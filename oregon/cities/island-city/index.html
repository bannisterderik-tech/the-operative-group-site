Island City, Oregon

Population about 1,144. Incorporated 1904. In Union County, seat at La Grande.

Short answer

Island City is an incorporated city in Union County, Oregon, with a population of about 1,144. It incorporated in 1904, making it the 158th largest of Oregon’s 241 cities. It sits below the 25,000-person HB 2001 middle-housing threshold, so local zoning governs what can be built on a residential lot.

Where it sits and what that decides

Island City sits in Union County, whose seat is La Grande. County business — recording, assessment, probate — happens there, not here.

Inside Union County, Island City is the 4th largest of 8 incorporated cities. With about 1,144 residents, Island City may see only a handful of recorded sales in a year. An appraiser will go outside the city limit to find comparables, and so will any honest price opinion.

Incorporated in 1904. Cities that formed in that window generally have a pre-war core and rings of later building around it, and the inspection findings change sharply between the two.

Getting your bearings

Island City sits at 45.3410, -118.0447, about 2.4 miles south-west of the county seat at La Grande. Eugene, where this team is based, is 263 miles west. It has no postal place name of its own, so mail is addressed through the surrounding area.

The nearest incorporated cities are La Grande (2.4 miles south-west), Imbler (9.3 miles north-east), Summerville (12 miles north) and Cove (12 miles east). Straight-line distances — the road takes longer, and in the Coast Range or the Cascades it can take much longer.

What Oregon law does in Island City

Under the 25,000-person line, so HB 2001 middle housing doesn’t reach it and city zoning decides. The 9.5% rent cap applies here as everywhere in Oregon. An estate is filed in Union County Circuit Court, at La Grande.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is Island City, Oregon in?

Union County. The county seat is La Grande, which is where recording, assessment and probate happen.

How big is Island City, Oregon?

About 1,144 people, which makes it the 158th largest of Oregon's 241 incorporated cities and the 4th largest of the 8 in Union County. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does HB 2001 middle housing apply in Island City?

No. Island City is below the 25,000-person threshold, so the full HB 2001 requirement doesn't reach it and the city's own zoning governs.

Where is probate filed for a Island City property?

In the Union County Circuit Court at La Grande. Venue follows the county, and your attorney confirms it before filing.
