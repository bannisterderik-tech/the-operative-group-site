Jacksonville, Oregon

Population about 3,020. Incorporated 1860. In Jackson County, seat at Medford.

Short answer

Jacksonville is an incorporated city in Jackson County, Oregon, with a population of about 3,020. It incorporated in 1860, making it the 101st largest of Oregon’s 241 cities. It sits below the 25,000-person HB 2001 middle-housing threshold, so local zoning governs what can be built on a residential lot.

Where it sits and what that decides

Jacksonville sits in Jackson County, whose seat is Medford. County business — recording, assessment, probate — happens there, not here.

Inside Jackson County, Jacksonville is the 8th largest of 11 incorporated cities. At around 3,020 people, Jacksonville is small enough that a single unusual sale moves the apparent median. Anybody pricing a house here should be reading individual sales, not averages.

It has been incorporated since 1860, which puts it among the oldest cities in the state — 8th oldest of the 241. Housing stock that old brings its own inspection list: original sewer laterals, unbolted foundations, knob-and-tube that somebody has partly replaced.

Getting your bearings

Jacksonville sits at 42.3135, -122.9670, about 4.8 miles east of the county seat at Medford. Eugene, where this team is based, is 120 miles north. Mail here is addressed 97530.

The nearest incorporated cities are Medford (4.8 miles east), Central Point (5.0 miles north-east), Phoenix (8.1 miles east) and Gold Hill (9.2 miles north-west). Straight-line distances — the road takes longer, and in the Coast Range or the Cascades it can take much longer.

What Oregon law does in Jacksonville

Under the 25,000-person line, so HB 2001 middle housing doesn’t reach it and city zoning decides. The 9.5% rent cap applies here as everywhere in Oregon. An estate is filed in Jackson County Circuit Court, at Medford.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is Jacksonville, Oregon in?

Jackson County. The county seat is Medford, which is where recording, assessment and probate happen.

How big is Jacksonville, Oregon?

About 3,020 people, which makes it the 101st largest of Oregon's 241 incorporated cities and the 8th largest of the 11 in Jackson County. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does HB 2001 middle housing apply in Jacksonville?

No. Jacksonville is below the 25,000-person threshold, so the full HB 2001 requirement doesn't reach it and the city's own zoning governs.

Where is probate filed for a Jacksonville property?

In the Jackson County Circuit Court at Medford. Venue follows the county, and your attorney confirms it before filing.
