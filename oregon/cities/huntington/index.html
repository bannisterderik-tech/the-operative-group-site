Huntington, Oregon

Population about 502. Incorporated 1891. In Baker County, seat at Baker City.

Short answer

Huntington is an incorporated city in Baker County, Oregon, with a population of about 502. It incorporated in 1891, making it the 193rd largest of Oregon’s 241 cities. It sits below the 25,000-person HB 2001 middle-housing threshold, so local zoning governs what can be built on a residential lot.

Where it sits and what that decides

Huntington sits in Baker County, whose seat is Baker City. County business — recording, assessment, probate — happens there, not here.

Inside Baker County, Huntington is the 2nd largest of 8 incorporated cities. With about 502 residents, Huntington may see only a handful of recorded sales in a year. An appraiser will go outside the city limit to find comparables, and so will any honest price opinion.

Incorporated in 1891. Cities that formed in that window generally have a pre-war core and rings of later building around it, and the inspection findings change sharply between the two.

Getting your bearings

Huntington sits at 44.3811, -117.3097, about 38 miles north-west of the county seat at Baker City. Eugene, where this team is based, is 287 miles west. Mail here is addressed 97907.

The nearest incorporated cities are Vale (28 miles south), Richland (29 miles north), Ontario (30 miles south-east) and Halfway (37 miles north). Straight-line distances — the road takes longer, and in the Coast Range or the Cascades it can take much longer.

What Oregon law does in Huntington

Under the 25,000-person line, so HB 2001 middle housing doesn’t reach it and city zoning decides. The 9.5% rent cap applies here as everywhere in Oregon. An estate is filed in Baker County Circuit Court, at Baker City.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is Huntington, Oregon in?

Baker County. The county seat is Baker City, which is where recording, assessment and probate happen.

How big is Huntington, Oregon?

About 502 people, which makes it the 193rd largest of Oregon's 241 incorporated cities and the 2nd largest of the 8 in Baker County. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does HB 2001 middle housing apply in Huntington?

No. Huntington is below the 25,000-person threshold, so the full HB 2001 requirement doesn't reach it and the city's own zoning governs.

Where is probate filed for a Huntington property?

In the Baker County Circuit Court at Baker City. Venue follows the county, and your attorney confirms it before filing.
