Barlow, Oregon

Population about 133. Incorporated 1903. In Clackamas County, seat at Oregon City.

Short answer

Barlow is an incorporated city in Clackamas County, Oregon, with a population of about 133. It incorporated in 1903, making it the 230th largest of Oregon’s 241 cities. It sits below the 25,000-person HB 2001 middle-housing threshold, so local zoning governs what can be built on a residential lot.

Where it sits and what that decides

Barlow sits in Clackamas County, whose seat is Oregon City. County business — recording, assessment, probate — happens there, not here.

Inside Clackamas County, Barlow is the 16th largest of 16 incorporated cities. Barlow is one of Oregon’s smallest incorporated cities, with about 133 residents. A sale here is a genuinely individual event; there’s no local market to average, and value gets argued from the county and the parcel instead of from a comp set.

Incorporated in 1903. Cities that formed in that window generally have a pre-war core and rings of later building around it, and the inspection findings change sharply between the two.

Getting your bearings

Barlow sits at 45.2522, -122.7225, about 9.2 miles north-east of the county seat at Oregon City. Eugene, where this team is based, is 85 miles south. It has no postal place name of its own, so mail is addressed through the surrounding area.

The nearest incorporated cities are Canby (1.6 miles north-east), Aurora (2.2 miles south-west), Wilsonville (4.1 miles north-west) and Donald (6.0 miles west). Straight-line distances — the road takes longer, and in the Coast Range or the Cascades it can take much longer.

What Oregon law does in Barlow

Under the 25,000-person line, so HB 2001 middle housing doesn’t reach it and city zoning decides. The 9.5% rent cap applies here as everywhere in Oregon. An estate is filed in Clackamas County Circuit Court, at Oregon City.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is Barlow, Oregon in?

Clackamas County. The county seat is Oregon City, which is where recording, assessment and probate happen.

How big is Barlow, Oregon?

About 133 people, which makes it the 230th largest of Oregon's 241 incorporated cities and the 16th largest of the 16 in Clackamas County. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does HB 2001 middle housing apply in Barlow?

No. Barlow is below the 25,000-person threshold, so the full HB 2001 requirement doesn't reach it and the city's own zoning governs.

Where is probate filed for a Barlow property?

In the Clackamas County Circuit Court at Oregon City. Venue follows the county, and your attorney confirms it before filing.
