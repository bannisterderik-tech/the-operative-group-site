Harney County

Burns is the county seat. Harney County was established in 1889 and its circuit court hears every probate and title action for property inside its lines.

Short answer

Harney County covers 10,135 square miles with about 7,380 residents, 2 incorporated cities and its county seat at Burns. Real estate here is governed by Oregon statute, so the rent cap, middle-housing rules and probate procedure are the same as in Eugene — and we place you here through the referral network.

The shape of the county

At 1 people per square mile this is frontier-density country. Comparable sales can be miles apart and months old, which changes how a price gets built: an appraiser here works from a much wider radius than one in Eugene, and so should you.

The county holds 2 incorporated cities, from Burns at 2,730 down to Hines at 1,645. That spread matters: which side of a city limit a parcel falls on decides its zoning, its utilities and often its taxes.

What Oregon law does here

No city in Harney County reaches the 25,000-person threshold that triggers the full HB 2001 middle-housing requirements, so local zoning governs what can be built on a residential lot here.

The statewide 9.5% rent cap under ORS 90.323 reaches Harney County exactly as it reaches Lane. Harney County sits in Oregon’s 24th judicial district, which it shares with Grant County. Cases are still filed in the Harney County Circuit Court at Burns — sharing a district means sharing judges and administration, not a courthouse. Probate in Harney County.

How much of the county is actually in a city

About 59% of Harney County’s residents live inside a city limit, which leaves roughly 3,005 people on unincorporated county land. That split is the single most practical thing to establish about a parcel here. Unincorporated property answers to the county planning department, usually sits on a well and a septic system, and is assessed by the county with no city levy on top.

The county’s oldest incorporated city is Burns, from 1891; the newest is Hines, from 1930. That 39-year spread is visible in the housing stock, and it is the first thing an inspection report tells you.

Asked and answered

Where is Harney County’s county seat?

Burns. The county seat holds the county offices and the Harney County Circuit Court, which is where probate and title actions for property in this county are filed.

How many people live in Harney County?

About 7,380, across 10,135 square miles — roughly 1 people per square mile. That makes it the 32nd most populous of Oregon’s 36 counties. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does the Oregon rent cap apply in Harney County?

Yes. ORS 90.323 is a statewide statute, so the 9.5% cap, the 15-year new-construction exemption and the 90-day notice requirement apply here the same way they apply in Eugene.
