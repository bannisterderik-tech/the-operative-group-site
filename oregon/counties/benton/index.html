Benton County

Corvallis is the county seat. Benton County was established in 1847 and its circuit court hears every probate and title action for property inside its lines.

Short answer

Benton County covers 676 square miles with about 97,728 residents, 5 incorporated cities and its county seat at Corvallis. Real estate here is governed by Oregon statute, so the rent cap, middle-housing rules and probate procedure are the same as in Eugene — and we place you here through the referral network.

The shape of the county

At 145 people per square mile, this is one of the denser counties in the state. Sales volume’s high enough that comparable sales are genuinely comparable, and pricing errors surface within a fortnight rather than a season.

The county holds 5 incorporated cities, from Corvallis at 59,922 down to Monroe at 647. That spread matters: which side of a city limit a parcel falls on decides its zoning, its utilities and often its taxes.

What Oregon law does here

Under HB 2001, cities above 25,000 people must allow duplexes, triplexes, quadplexes and more in their residential zones. In Benton County that means Corvallis and Albany. Everywhere else in the county, the city’s own zoning still governs.

The statewide 9.5% rent cap under ORS 90.323 reaches Benton County exactly as it reaches Lane. Benton County is the whole of Oregon’s 21st judicial district. Its circuit court sits at Corvallis, and every probate, partition and quiet-title action for property in this county is filed there. Probate in Benton County.

How much of the county is actually in a city

About 68% of Benton County’s residents live inside a city limit, which leaves roughly 30,815 people on unincorporated county land. That split is the single most practical thing to establish about a parcel here. Unincorporated property answers to the county planning department, usually sits on a well and a septic system, and is assessed by the county with no city levy on top.

The county’s oldest incorporated city is Corvallis, from 1857; the newest is Adair Village, from 1976. That 119-year spread is visible in the housing stock, and it is the first thing an inspection report tells you.

8 postal codes cover the county, spread across roughly 21 miles north to south and 17 miles east to west. Every Oregon postal code is indexed by county.

Questions

Asked and answered

Where is Benton County’s county seat?

Corvallis. The county seat holds the county offices and the Benton County Circuit Court, which is where probate and title actions for property in this county are filed.

How many people live in Benton County?

About 97,728, across 676 square miles — roughly 145 people per square mile. That makes it the 11th most populous of Oregon’s 36 counties. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does the Oregon rent cap apply in Benton County?

Yes. ORS 90.323 is a statewide statute, so the 9.5% cap, the 15-year new-construction exemption and the 90-day notice requirement apply here the same way they apply in Eugene.
