Multnomah County

Portland is the county seat. Multnomah County was established in 1854 and its circuit court hears every probate and title action for property inside its lines.

Short answer

Multnomah County covers 435 square miles with about 795,391 residents, 8 incorporated cities and its county seat at Portland. Real estate here is governed by Oregon statute, so the rent cap, middle-housing rules and probate procedure are the same as in Eugene — and we place you here through the referral network.

The shape of the county

At 1,828 people per square mile, this is one of the denser counties in the state. Sales volume’s high enough that comparable sales are genuinely comparable, and pricing errors surface within a fortnight rather than a season.

The county holds 8 incorporated cities, from Portland at 652,503 down to Maywood Park at 829. That spread matters: which side of a city limit a parcel falls on decides its zoning, its utilities and often its taxes.

What Oregon law does here

Under HB 2001, cities above 25,000 people must allow duplexes, triplexes, quadplexes and more in their residential zones. In Multnomah County that means Portland, Gresham and Lake Oswego. Everywhere else in the county, the city’s own zoning still governs.

The statewide 9.5% rent cap under ORS 90.323 reaches Multnomah County exactly as it reaches Lane. Multnomah County is the whole of Oregon’s 4th judicial district. Its circuit court sits at Portland, and every probate, partition and quiet-title action for property in this county is filed there. Probate in Multnomah County.

How much of the county is actually in a city

About 100% of Multnomah County’s residents live inside a city limit, which leaves roughly 0 people on unincorporated county land. That split is the single most practical thing to establish about a parcel here. Unincorporated property answers to the county planning department, usually sits on a well and a septic system, and is assessed by the county with no city levy on top.

The county’s oldest incorporated city is Portland, from 1851; the newest is Maywood Park, from 1967. That 116-year spread is visible in the housing stock, and it is the first thing an inspection report tells you.

56 postal codes cover the county, spread across roughly 14 miles north to south and 36 miles east to west. Every Oregon postal code is indexed by county.

Questions

Asked and answered

Where is Multnomah County’s county seat?

Portland. The county seat holds the county offices and the Multnomah County Circuit Court, which is where probate and title actions for property in this county are filed.

How many people live in Multnomah County?

About 795,391, across 435 square miles — roughly 1,828 people per square mile. That makes it the 1st most populous of Oregon’s 36 counties. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does the Oregon rent cap apply in Multnomah County?

Yes. ORS 90.323 is a statewide statute, so the 9.5% cap, the 15-year new-construction exemption and the 90-day notice requirement apply here the same way they apply in Eugene.
