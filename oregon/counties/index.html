Oregon’s thirty-six counties

Seat, population, area and circuit court for all thirty-six, plus every one of Oregon’s 241 incorporated cities sorted into them.

Short answer

Oregon has 36 counties and 241 incorporated cities. The largest by population is Multnomah at 795,391; the smallest is Wheeler at 1,472. The largest by area is Harney at 10,135 square miles. Probate and title actions are filed in the circuit court for the county where the property sits.

Oregon runs land use, landlord–tenant law and probate at the state level, which means most of what governs a transaction’s identical from Lakeview to Astoria. What changes county by county is the court you file in, the planning department that issues the permit, and how far apart the comparable sales are. Those three things are what these pages carry.

Questions

Asked and answered

How many counties does Oregon have?

Thirty-six. The oldest were established in 1843 — Clackamas, Marion, Washington and Yamhill — and the newest is Deschutes in 1916.

Which Oregon county is the biggest?

By population, Multnomah, with about 795,391 residents in only 435 square miles. By land area, Harney, at 10,135 square miles and about 7,380 people.

Which county do I file probate in?

The circuit court for the county where the decedent lived, with the property’s location also relevant. Oregon has 36 county circuit courts grouped into 27 judicial districts. Your attorney confirms venue before anything is filed.
