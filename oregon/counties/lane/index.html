Lane County

Eugene is the county seat. Lane County was established in 1851 and its circuit court hears every probate and title action for property inside its lines.

Short answer

Lane County covers 4,554 square miles with about 381,584 residents, 12 incorporated cities and its county seat at Eugene. Real estate here is governed by Oregon statute, so the rent cap, middle-housing rules and probate procedure are the same as in Eugene — and this is the county the team works every day.

The shape of the county

At 84 people per square mile, this is one of the denser counties in the state. Sales volume’s high enough that comparable sales are genuinely comparable, and pricing errors surface within a fortnight rather than a season.

The county holds 12 incorporated cities, from Eugene at 176,654 down to Westfir at 259. That spread matters: which side of a city limit a parcel falls on decides its zoning, its utilities and often its taxes.

What Oregon law does here

Under HB 2001, cities above 25,000 people must allow duplexes, triplexes, quadplexes and more in their residential zones. In Lane County that means Eugene and Springfield. Everywhere else in the county, the city’s own zoning still governs.

The statewide 9.5% rent cap under ORS 90.323 reaches Lane County exactly as it reaches Lane. Lane County is the whole of Oregon’s 2nd judicial district. Its circuit court sits at Eugene, and every probate, partition and quiet-title action for property in this county is filed there. Probate in Lane County.

How much of the county is actually in a city

About 74% of Lane County’s residents live inside a city limit, which leaves roughly 98,072 people on unincorporated county land. That split is the single most practical thing to establish about a parcel here. Unincorporated property answers to the county planning department, usually sits on a well and a septic system, and is assessed by the county with no city levy on top.

The county’s oldest incorporated city is Eugene, from 1862; the newest is Westfir, from 1979. That 117-year spread is visible in the housing stock, and it is the first thing an inspection report tells you.

37 postal codes cover the county, spread across roughly 32 miles north to south and 98 miles east to west. Every Oregon postal code is indexed by county.

Questions

Asked and answered

Where is Lane County’s county seat?

Eugene. The county seat holds the county offices and the Lane County Circuit Court, which is where probate and title actions for property in this county are filed.

How many people live in Lane County?

About 381,584, across 4,554 square miles — roughly 84 people per square mile. That makes it the 4th most populous of Oregon’s 36 counties. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does the Oregon rent cap apply in Lane County?

Yes. ORS 90.323 is a statewide statute, so the 9.5% cap, the 15-year new-construction exemption and the 90-day notice requirement apply here the same way they apply in Eugene.

Do you work in Lane County?

Yes — Lane County is the home market. The team is based in Eugene and works this county every day.
