Umatilla County

Pendleton is the county seat. Umatilla County was established in 1862 and its circuit court hears every probate and title action for property inside its lines.

Short answer

Umatilla County covers 3,215 square miles with about 81,119 residents, 12 incorporated cities and its county seat at Pendleton. Real estate here is governed by Oregon statute, so the rent cap, middle-housing rules and probate procedure are the same as in Eugene — and we place you here through the referral network.

The shape of the county

At 25 people per square mile, Umatilla County is rural by any measure. There are usually enough recent sales to build a price from, but not enough to be casual about which ones you pick.

The county holds 12 incorporated cities, from Hermiston at 19,354 down to Ukiah at 159. That spread matters: which side of a city limit a parcel falls on decides its zoning, its utilities and often its taxes.

What Oregon law does here

No city in Umatilla County reaches the 25,000-person threshold that triggers the full HB 2001 middle-housing requirements, so local zoning governs what can be built on a residential lot here.

The statewide 9.5% rent cap under ORS 90.323 reaches Umatilla County exactly as it reaches Lane. Umatilla County sits in Oregon’s 6th judicial district, which it shares with Morrow County. Cases are still filed in the Umatilla County Circuit Court at Pendleton — sharing a district means sharing judges and administration, not a courthouse. Probate in Umatilla County.

How much of the county is actually in a city

About 71% of Umatilla County’s residents live inside a city limit, which leaves roughly 23,383 people on unincorporated county land. That split is the single most practical thing to establish about a parcel here. Unincorporated property answers to the county planning department, usually sits on a well and a septic system, and is assessed by the county with no city levy on top.

The county’s oldest incorporated city is Umatilla, from 1864; the newest is Ukiah, from 1969. That 105-year spread is visible in the housing stock, and it is the first thing an inspection report tells you.

13 postal codes cover the county, spread across roughly 60 miles north to south and 47 miles east to west. Every Oregon postal code is indexed by county.

Questions

Asked and answered

Where is Umatilla County’s county seat?

Pendleton. The county seat holds the county offices and the Umatilla County Circuit Court, which is where probate and title actions for property in this county are filed.

How many people live in Umatilla County?

About 81,119, across 3,215 square miles — roughly 25 people per square mile. That makes it the 14th most populous of Oregon’s 36 counties. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does the Oregon rent cap apply in Umatilla County?

Yes. ORS 90.323 is a statewide statute, so the 9.5% cap, the 15-year new-construction exemption and the 90-day notice requirement apply here the same way they apply in Eugene.
