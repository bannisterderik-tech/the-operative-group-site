Union County

La Grande is the county seat. Union County was established in 1864 and its circuit court hears every probate and title action for property inside its lines.

Short answer

Union County covers 2,037 square miles with about 25,900 residents, 8 incorporated cities and its county seat at La Grande. Real estate here is governed by Oregon statute, so the rent cap, middle-housing rules and probate procedure are the same as in Eugene — and we place you here through the referral network.

The shape of the county

At 13 people per square mile, Union County is rural by any measure. There are usually enough recent sales to build a price from, but not enough to be casual about which ones you pick.

The county holds 8 incorporated cities, from La Grande at 13,026 down to Summerville at 119. That spread matters: which side of a city limit a parcel falls on decides its zoning, its utilities and often its taxes.

What Oregon law does here

No city in Union County reaches the 25,000-person threshold that triggers the full HB 2001 middle-housing requirements, so local zoning governs what can be built on a residential lot here.

The statewide 9.5% rent cap under ORS 90.323 reaches Union County exactly as it reaches Lane. Union County sits in Oregon’s 10th judicial district, which it shares with Wallowa County. Cases are still filed in the Union County Circuit Court at La Grande — sharing a district means sharing judges and administration, not a courthouse. Probate in Union County.

How much of the county is actually in a city

About 75% of Union County’s residents live inside a city limit, which leaves roughly 6,373 people on unincorporated county land. That split is the single most practical thing to establish about a parcel here. Unincorporated property answers to the county planning department, usually sits on a well and a septic system, and is assessed by the county with no city levy on top.

The county’s oldest incorporated city is La Grande, from 1865; the newest is Imbler, from 1922. That 57-year spread is visible in the housing stock, and it is the first thing an inspection report tells you.

7 postal codes cover the county, spread across roughly 39 miles north to south and 13 miles east to west. Every Oregon postal code is indexed by county.

Questions

Asked and answered

Where is Union County’s county seat?

La Grande. The county seat holds the county offices and the Union County Circuit Court, which is where probate and title actions for property in this county are filed.

How many people live in Union County?

About 25,900, across 2,037 square miles — roughly 13 people per square mile. That makes it the 24th most populous of Oregon’s 36 counties. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does the Oregon rent cap apply in Union County?

Yes. ORS 90.323 is a statewide statute, so the 9.5% cap, the 15-year new-construction exemption and the 90-day notice requirement apply here the same way they apply in Eugene.
