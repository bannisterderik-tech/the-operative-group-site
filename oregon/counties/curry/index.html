Curry County

Gold Beach is the county seat. Curry County was established in 1855 and its circuit court hears every probate and title action for property inside its lines.

Short answer

Curry County covers 1,627 square miles with about 22,621 residents, 3 incorporated cities and its county seat at Gold Beach. Real estate here is governed by Oregon statute, so the rent cap, middle-housing rules and probate procedure are the same as in Eugene — and we place you here through the referral network.

The shape of the county

At 14 people per square mile, Curry County is rural by any measure. There are usually enough recent sales to build a price from, but not enough to be casual about which ones you pick.

The county holds 3 incorporated cities, from Brookings at 6,744 down to Port Orford at 1,146. That spread matters: which side of a city limit a parcel falls on decides its zoning, its utilities and often its taxes.

What Oregon law does here

No city in Curry County reaches the 25,000-person threshold that triggers the full HB 2001 middle-housing requirements, so local zoning governs what can be built on a residential lot here.

The statewide 9.5% rent cap under ORS 90.323 reaches Curry County exactly as it reaches Lane. Curry County sits in Oregon’s 15th judicial district, which it shares with Coos County. Cases are still filed in the Curry County Circuit Court at Gold Beach — sharing a district means sharing judges and administration, not a courthouse. Probate in Curry County.

How much of the county is actually in a city

About 45% of Curry County’s residents live inside a city limit, which leaves roughly 12,390 people on unincorporated county land. That split is the single most practical thing to establish about a parcel here. Unincorporated property answers to the county planning department, usually sits on a well and a septic system, and is assessed by the county with no city levy on top.

The county’s oldest incorporated city is Port Orford, from 1935; the newest is Brookings, from 1951. That 16-year spread is visible in the housing stock, and it is the first thing an inspection report tells you.

Questions

Asked and answered

Where is Curry County’s county seat?

Gold Beach. The county seat holds the county offices and the Curry County Circuit Court, which is where probate and title actions for property in this county are filed.

How many people live in Curry County?

About 22,621, across 1,627 square miles — roughly 14 people per square mile. That makes it the 27th most populous of Oregon’s 36 counties. Figures retrieved September 2026.

Does the Oregon rent cap apply in Curry County?

Yes. ORS 90.323 is a statewide statute, so the 9.5% cap, the 15-year new-construction exemption and the 90-day notice requirement apply here the same way they apply in Eugene.
