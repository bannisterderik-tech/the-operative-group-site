Riddle SD 70

2 schools, about 344 students, PK through 12. District office in Riddle.

Short answer

Riddle SD 70 is a public school district in Douglas County, Oregon, running 2 schools for about 344 students across PK through 12. Its office is in Riddle. District boundaries do not follow city limits or postal codes, so confirm the district for a specific address with the district itself before you rely on it.

The size of it

With about 344 students this is one of the smallest districts in the state, 132nd of 173. Districts this size cover a lot of ground for very few families, and the drive matters more than any line on a map.

2 schools and 344 students works out at roughly 172 per school on average, though averages hide a lot: an elementary and a high school in the same district rarely resemble each other.

Finding out which district an address is in

Not from a postal code, and not from a city limit. Oregon district boundaries were drawn around where children actually were, over more than a century, and they cross both constantly. Douglas County alone has 9 districts in it, and a single street can have two of them on opposite sides.

The district office is the authority on its own boundary. Call it with the address. If you’re buying, do that before you write the offer, not after — and put nothing in the contract that depends on an assumption about it.

Ask the district, visit the schools, and make that call yourself.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is Riddle SD 70 in?

Douglas County, Oregon. The district office is in Riddle. A district office sitting in one city doesn’t mean the district stops at that city’s limits.

How many schools does Riddle SD 70 have?

2, serving about 344 students across PK through 12. Figures are from the federal Common Core of Data for 2022.

How do I find out if a house is in Riddle SD 70?

Ask the district office. Boundaries do not follow postal codes or city limits, and no third-party map is authoritative. Confirm it before you write an offer if it matters to you.

Do you rank the schools?

No. Ranking schools alongside property listings steers buyers, and steering on familial status or race breaches the Fair Housing Act regardless of intent. We will tell you which district an address is in and leave the judgment to you.
