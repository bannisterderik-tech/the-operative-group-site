Mt Angel SD 91

3 schools, about 670 students, PK through 12. District office in Mt Angel.

Short answer

Mt Angel SD 91 is a public school district in Marion County, Oregon, running 3 schools for about 670 students across PK through 12. Its office is in Mt Angel. District boundaries do not follow city limits or postal codes, so confirm the district for a specific address with the district itself before you rely on it.

The size of it

About 670 students across 3 schools makes this a small district, 112th of 173. At this size there’s often one school per grade band and no internal attendance boundary to worry about.

3 schools and 670 students works out at roughly 223 per school on average, though averages hide a lot: an elementary and a high school in the same district rarely resemble each other.

Finding out which district an address is in

Not from a postal code, and not from a city limit. Oregon district boundaries were drawn around where children actually were, over more than a century, and they cross both constantly. Marion County alone has 11 districts in it, and a single street can have two of them on opposite sides.

The district office is the authority on its own boundary. Call it with the address. If you’re buying, do that before you write the offer, not after — and put nothing in the contract that depends on an assumption about it.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is Mt Angel SD 91 in?

Marion County, Oregon. The district office is in Mt Angel. A district office sitting in one city doesn’t mean the district stops at that city’s limits.

How many schools does Mt Angel SD 91 have?

3, serving about 670 students across PK through 12. Figures are from the federal Common Core of Data for 2022.

How do I find out if a house is in Mt Angel SD 91?

Ask the district office. Boundaries do not follow postal codes or city limits, and no third-party map is authoritative. Confirm it before you write an offer if it matters to you.

Do you rank the schools?

No. Ranking schools alongside property listings steers buyers, and steering on familial status or race breaches the Fair Housing Act regardless of intent. We will tell you which district an address is in and leave the judgment to you.
