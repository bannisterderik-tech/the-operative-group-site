Morrow County school district

Morrow SD 1

9 schools, about 2,318 students, PK through 12. District office in Irrigon.

Short answer

Morrow SD 1 is a public school district in Morrow County, Oregon, running 9 schools for about 2,318 students across PK through 12. Its office is in Irrigon. District boundaries do not follow city limits or postal codes, so confirm the district for a specific address with the district itself before you rely on it.

Urban Institute Education Data Portal / NCES Common Core of Data, 2022, retrieved September 2026

Checked September 2026

The size of it

About 2,318 students across 9 schools makes this a small district, 59th of 173. At this size there’s often one school per grade band and no internal attendance boundary to worry about.

9 schools and 2,318 students works out at roughly 258 per school on average, though averages hide a lot: an elementary and a high school in the same district rarely resemble each other.

Finding out which district an address is in

Not from a postal code, and not from a city limit. Oregon district boundaries were drawn around where children actually were, over more than a century, and they cross both constantly. Morrow County alone has 1 district in it, and a single street can have two of them on opposite sides.

The district office is the authority on its own boundary. Call it with the address. If you’re buying, do that before you write the offer, not after — and put nothing in the contract that depends on an assumption about it.

No ratings on this siteWe publish which district a school is in, not whether it’s any good. Ranking schools next to property listings is how a brokerage steers buyers, and steering on familial status or race is unlawful under the Fair Housing Act whether or not anybody meant it. Ask the district, visit the schools, and make that call yourself.
Directory data, not a boundary map. Enrolment and school counts come from the federal Common Core of Data for 2022 and move every year. Districts consolidate. Verify with the district.
Questions

Asked and answered

What county is Morrow SD 1 in?

Morrow County, Oregon. The district office is in Irrigon. A district office sitting in one city doesn’t mean the district stops at that city’s limits.

How many schools does Morrow SD 1 have?

9, serving about 2,318 students across PK through 12. Figures are from the federal Common Core of Data for 2022.

How do I find out if a house is in Morrow SD 1?

Ask the district office. Boundaries do not follow postal codes or city limits, and no third-party map is authoritative. Confirm it before you write an offer if it matters to you.

Do you rank the schools?

No. Ranking schools alongside property listings steers buyers, and steering on familial status or race breaches the Fair Housing Act regardless of intent. We will tell you which district an address is in and leave the judgment to you.
