97526 — Grants Pass

An Oregon postal code in Josephine County, covering the city of Grants Pass. Recorded and adjudicated at Grants Pass.

Short answer

97526 is an Oregon postal code for Grants Pass, in Josephine County. Grants Pass is an incorporated city of about 39,189 people. Deeds are recorded and probate is filed at the county seat, Grants Pass, about 1.9 miles south-east.

Checked September 2026

What this postal code does and doesn’t decide

A postal code is a delivery route, not a jurisdiction. 97526 tells the post office how to sort mail. Zoning, the recorder who holds your deed and the court that hears a probate all follow the county line instead, and sometimes a city limit running straight through the middle of a postal code. Where the two disagree — and in Oregon they often do — the county and the city win.

Grants Pass is an incorporated city, population about 39,189, incorporated in 1887. It’s served by 3 postal codes in total, so 97526 covers part of the city and not all of it. The Grants Pass page has population, zoning thresholds and the court.

The rules that reach an address in 97526

Oregon runs landlord–tenant law, land use and probate at the state level, so a rental in 97526 is under the same 9.5% increase cap as one in Portland — ORS 90.323, with buildings under 15 years old exempt and 90 days’ notice required. The arithmetic.

Because Grants Pass is above 25,000 people, HB 2001 middle housing applies to its residential zones, so a lot in 97526 inside the city limit may carry more than a single detached house.

An estate holding real property in 97526 is filed in the Josephine County Circuit Court at Grants Pass, whatever the mailing address says. Probate in Josephine County.

Postal data, not a boundary map. Postal codes are drawn by USPS for delivery and are redrawn without notice. Confirm the county and the city limit for a specific parcel with the county assessor first.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is 97526 in?

Josephine County, Oregon. The county seat is Grants Pass, which is where deeds are recorded and where the circuit court sits.

Is Grants Pass, Oregon an incorporated city?

Yes. Grants Pass incorporated in 1887 and has a population of about 39,189.

Does a ZIP code determine my school district or city limit?

No. Postal codes are USPS delivery routes. City limits, county lines and school district boundaries get drawn separately, and all three frequently cut straight across a postal code. Verify a specific address with the county assessor and the district.

Where would probate be filed for a property in 97526?

The Josephine County Circuit Court at Grants Pass. Venue follows the county the property sits in and where the decedent lived, not the mailing address.
