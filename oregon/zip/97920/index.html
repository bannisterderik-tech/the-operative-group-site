97920 — Westfall

An Oregon postal code in Malheur County, covering the Westfall area. Recorded and adjudicated at Vale.

Short answer

97920 is an Oregon postal code for Westfall, in Malheur County. Westfall has no city government — it’s a named postal place, so the county sets land use for it. Deeds are recorded and probate is filed at the county seat, Vale, about 22 miles east.

What this postal code does and doesn’t decide

A postal code is a delivery route, not a jurisdiction. 97920 tells the post office how to sort mail. Zoning, the recorder who holds your deed and the court that hears a probate all follow the county line instead, and sometimes a city limit running straight through the middle of a postal code. Where the two disagree — and in Oregon they often do — the county and the city win.

Westfall has a postal place name but no city government. Land-use questions for an address in 97920 go to Malheur County planning, not to a city hall, and the statewide rules apply directly with no local overlay in between.

Questions

Asked and answered

What county is 97920 in?

Malheur County, Oregon. The county seat is Vale, which is where deeds are recorded and where the circuit court sits.

Is Westfall, Oregon an incorporated city?

No. Westfall is a named postal place without a city government. Land use for an address here is administered by Malheur County.

Does a ZIP code determine my school district or city limit?

No. Postal codes are USPS delivery routes. City limits, county lines and school district boundaries get drawn separately, and all three frequently cut straight across a postal code. Verify a specific address with the county assessor and the district.

Where would probate be filed for a property in 97920?

The Malheur County Circuit Court at Vale. Venue follows the county the property sits in and where the decedent lived, not the mailing address.
