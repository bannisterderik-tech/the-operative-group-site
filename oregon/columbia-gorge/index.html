The Columbia Gorge & Hood River

A National Scenic Area, which means land-use rules that exist almost nowhere else in the state.

We can place you hereWorked through the team and REAL Broker’s referral network of nearly 10,000 agents.

What is here

Counties. Hood River (seat: Hood River) · Wasco (seat: The Dalles)

Principal cities. Hood River · The Dalles · Cascade Locks · Mosier · Dufur

What matters here

  • Hood River is the Hood River County seat; The Dalles is the Wasco County seat
  • The Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area imposes a review layer on development that sits on top of normal county zoning
  • Orchard land and agricultural zoning restrict dwellings on a large share of the county’s acreage
  • Rainfall drops sharply from west to east across the Gorge, which changes both construction and landscaping economics within a thirty-mile drive

The rules that follow you here

Oregon sets rent increases, middle housing and probate at the state level, so most of what governs a transaction in Columbia Gorge is identical to what governs one in Eugene. That’s genuinely useful: the reference library on this site works for any Oregon address.

Which counties does Columbia Gorge cover?

Hood River County, seat Hood River; Wasco County, seat The Dalles. County seat matters more than people expect — it’s where probate is filed and where county land-use decisions are made.

Does the Oregon rent cap apply in Columbia Gorge?

Yes. ORS 90.323 is statewide and for 2026 the maximum increase is 9.5% for most long-term residential rentals, 6% for manufactured home facilities and marinas with more than 30 spaces. Properties less than 15 years old are exempt. A local rule can be stricter than the state floor but never looser.

What about probate on a property in Columbia Gorge?

Oregon probate is state law, so the thresholds are identical everywhere: a small estate affidavit is available at $275,000 or less with no more than $200,000 in real property and $75,000 in personal property, and it can’t be filed until 30 days after the date of death. What changes by region is which circuit court you file in — Hood River County files in Hood River, Wasco County files in The Dalles.
