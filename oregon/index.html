Eight rules that follow
your address anywhere.

Rent caps, middle housing, probate thresholds and sale authority are set by the state, not by the county. These apply in Astoria and in Ontario and everywhere between.

Maximum annual rent increase, 2026
9.5%
most long-term residential rentals, ORS 90.323; 6% for manufactured home facilities and marinas over 30 spaces; properties under 15 years old are exempt · Oregon DAS Office of Economic Analysis
Middle housing — cities over 25,000
4-plex
must allow duplexes, triplexes, quadplexes, townhouses and cottage clusters where detached single-family homes are allowed (HB 2001) · Oregon DLCD
Middle housing — cities 10,000 to 25,000
duplex
must allow a duplex on every lot where a detached single-family home is allowed · Oregon DLCD
Middle housing — inside Metro
1,000
inside the Portland Metro urban growth boundary the requirement reaches every city over 1,000 people · Oregon DLCD
Small estate affidavit ceiling
$275,000
no more than $200,000 real property and $75,000 personal property; cannot be filed until 30 days after death · Oregon Judicial Department
Creditor claim period
4 months
from first publication of notice to interested persons, ORS 115.005 — the floor under any Oregon probate timeline · ORS 115.005
Selling real property in probate
ORS 114.325
unless the will provides otherwise, the personal representative may sell or convey without a court hearing, with stated exceptions · Oregon Revised Statutes
Rent increase notice
90 days
written, once per twelve months, and never during the first year of a tenancy · ORS 90.323

City population tiers under HB 2001 move as populations do. Confirm your city's current tier with the Oregon Department of Land Conservation and Development before you rely on it. None of this is legal advice.
