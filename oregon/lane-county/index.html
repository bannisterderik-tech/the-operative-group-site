Questions

Asked and answered

Do you work in Lane County?

Yes — this is home. The team is based in Eugene and works Lane County every day, from Florence on the coast to McKenzie Bridge below the pass.

Which counties does Lane County cover?

Lane County, seat Eugene. County seat matters more than people expect — it is where probate is filed and where county land-use decisions are made.

Does the Oregon rent cap apply in Lane County?

Yes. ORS 90.323 is statewide and for 2026 the maximum increase is 9.5% for most long-term residential rentals, 6% for manufactured home facilities and marinas with more than 30 spaces. Properties less than 15 years old are exempt. A local rule can be stricter than the state floor but never looser.

What about probate on a property in Lane County?

Oregon probate is state law, so the thresholds are identical everywhere: a small estate affidavit is available at $275,000 or less with no more than $200,000 in real property and $75,000 in personal property, and it cannot be filed until 30 days after the date of death. What changes by region is which circuit court you file in — Lane County files in Eugene.
