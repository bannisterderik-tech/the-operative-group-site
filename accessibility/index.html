Accessibility

How theoperativegroup.com is built for keyboard, screen reader and reduced-motion use, where it may still fall short, and how to tell us so we can fix it.

What we have done

This site is built to be used with a keyboard, with a screen reader, and at any text size. Specifically: every page has one heading structure that makes sense read aloud, every informative image has alternative text, focus is always visible, color contrast meets WCAG 2.1 AA on text, motion is disabled entirely when your system asks for reduced motion, and no information is conveyed by color alone.

Where it may still fall short

The calculators are interactive and were built with keyboard and screen reader use in mind, but interactive tools are the hardest part of any site to get right. Content on third-party sites we link to is outside our control.

Tell us

If any part of this site is difficult or impossible for you to use, call 458.209.0163 or email dan@theoperativegroup.com. Describe what happened and what you were trying to do. We will fix it, and in the meantime we will give you the information you were after another way.
