Questions

Asked and answered

Where does 9.5% come from?

The Oregon DAS Office of Economic Analysis publishes the figure by September 30 each year, for the following calendar year. The formula is seven percent plus the twelve-month average change in CPI-U West Region (All Items), or ten percent, whichever is lower.

Which properties are exempt?

Properties less than 15 years old. The clock runs from the certificate of occupancy.

How much notice is required?

At least 90 days in writing. A missed notice period voids the increase rather than delaying it.

Can I raise rent twice in a year?

No. Rent may be increased only once in any twelve-month period, and never during the first year of a tenancy.

Does a city rule override this?

No. The statewide law supersedes local regulations that are less restrictive.
