Eugene ADU size checker

Eugene allows an ADU in all residential zones. A detached ADU is capped at 800 square feet or 10% of the lot area, whichever is smaller — and on most Eugene lots it’s the percentage that binds, not the 800.

This applies the published size cap only. Setbacks, height, parking, lot coverage, tree rules, utility connections and site access all still govern what can be built, and city code changes. Confirm with the City of Eugene planning division for your specific address before you spend money on plans.

Questions

Asked and answered

Do I have to live on the property?

No. Owner occupancy isn’t required in Eugene, which means both the main house and the ADU can be rented.

Which rule usually applies?

The 10% rule, on most lots. Ten percent of a lot only reaches 800 square feet at 8,000 square feet of lot area — roughly a fifth of an acre.

Is zoning permission the same as feasibility?

No. Setbacks, parking, utility connections, tree preservation and site access all still apply, and any of them can make a legally permitted ADU physically impractical or expensive.

What about HOA rules?

Under HB 2138, private deed restrictions and HOA covenants that banned duplexes, triplexes or ADUs are retroactively void — that provision takes effect January 1, 2027.

Will a lender count the rent from an ADU?

Not from one that doesn’t exist yet. Existing, leased units are treated differently. Ask your lender specifically.
