Questions

Asked and answered

Why compare two scenarios?

Because the highest gross price and the highest net are frequently not the same deal. A listing that sells for more but takes repairs, concessions, and four extra months of carrying cost can lose to a lower as-is number.

What should I use for commission?

Commission is negotiable and is not set by law or by any association. Use the figure in your actual agreement. Since 2024 buyer-side compensation is negotiated separately and may appear as a seller concession instead.

What are typical closing costs for a seller?

Title, escrow, recording, any county or transfer fees, prorated taxes, and anything you agree to pay on the buyer’s behalf. Your escrow officer gives you the real figure — this is an estimate.

Does this include capital gains tax?

No. Whether you owe tax on a sale depends on your basis, how long you held it, whether it was your primary residence and other factors. Ask your CPA.
