Sell a house in
Eugene

County seat of Lane County and home of the University of Oregon.

Eugene isn’t one market. A 1920s bungalow in Jefferson Westside, a hillside contemporary in the Southwest Hills, and a 1978 ranch in Bethel each want a different exit, a different price strategy, and a different buyer pool.

What Eugene is

County seat of Lane County and home of the University of Oregon. Everything from pre-war bungalows near downtown to 1990s–2010s subdivisions in Santa Clara and southwest.

  • Lane County seat; the Willamette and McKenzie rivers meet just north of town
  • Split between two school districts — Eugene 4J and Bethel
  • Five public high schools inside the city
  • Interstate 5 on the east side, Highway 99 north, Highway 126 running east to the McKenzie and west to the coast

What an inspector finds here

Eugene's stock spans a century, so the inspection depends entirely on the decade. Pre-war grid houses bring clay sewer laterals and unbolted foundations; hillside houses bring drainage and retaining walls; 1970s subdivisions bring panels and furnaces at end of life.

Who is buying

Every buyer type in the county is here at once — first-time buyers on government-backed loans, move-up buyers, relocation buyers shopping from out of state, and investors underwriting on rent.

Selling here

With the deepest buyer pool in the county, a prepared RMLS listing usually produces the highest gross price in Eugene. The reason to run the other three anyway is timing and condition: a house that needs work, or a seller who needs a date, can net more from an off-market or portfolio sale once repairs and carrying cost come out.

When this market moves

Eugene's market wakes up in late February and runs hard through June, with a second, quieter window in September. A house that sits past Labor Day competes with holiday distraction.

The market behind the number

Lane County's median sale price ran about $464,000 for the three months ending June 2026, down 3.5% from a year earlier, with a median of 18 days on market (Redfin). Eugene is the deepest and most measurable market in the county — enough sales close each month to produce a median that means something. It’s also the widest price band inside one city name, which is why a citywide figure is the wrong tool for pricing a specific address.

What trips up Eugene sales

Pre-1978 homes bring lead-based paint disclosure obligations and, on FHA and VA loans, repair requirements.
Middle-housing and ADU potential is a value driver an appraiser won’t volunteer — it has to be marketed.
An automated valuation can’t see condition, access or view — on most Lane County property it’s a starting point, not a number.
Questions

Asked and answered

How fast do houses sell in Eugene?

County-wide the median was 18 days on market in June 2026, down from 22 a year earlier (Redfin). A city like Eugene tracks closer to that number than the rural corridors do. The honest answer for your address comes from a CMA, not from an average.

What is my Eugene house worth?

An automated estimate is a starting point and nothing more — it can’t see condition, view, access, or what the last three comparable sales actually closed at. We run a comparative market analysis on the address and, at the same time, price all four exits so you can see the spread.

Can I sell in Eugene without listing it publicly?

Yes. Exit 02 is an off-market sale to our curated list of more than 700 accredited and cash investors — no sign, no public listing, no showings. It typically nets less than a full RMLS listing, and we tell you how much less before you choose it.

Do you work outside Eugene and Springfield?

All of Lane County, and beyond it all of Oregon. Each city in the county has its own school district, its own buyer pool and its own pricing logic, and we price to the town rather than to a county average.

What is the first thing I should do if I am selling in Eugene?

Decide which of the four exits you’re testing before you prepare anything, because the preparation for an RMLS listing and for an as-is sale are different jobs.

What does it cost to sell a house in Eugene?

Commission, title and escrow, any concessions you agree to, and the repairs you choose to make. On Eugene property the repair line is the variable one, which is why the inspection profile above matters to your net. Commissions are negotiable and are not set by law or by any association, and ours is paid at closing out of the proceeds — there’s nothing to pay up front. The number to compare between offers is net proceeds, not gross price.
