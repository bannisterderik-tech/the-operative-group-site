What Blue River is

Upper McKenzie community, most of which burned in 2020. New construction on burned lots, bare land, surviving houses, and land-trust homes.

  • McKenzie School District
  • Blue River Reservoir and the Willamette National Forest are minutes away
  • Most of the community burned in the 2020 Holiday Farm Fire and is being rebuilt
  • Unincorporated Lane County — county land use and permitting
Rebuilding marketThe Holiday Farm Fire burned 173,000+ acres September 7 to October 29, 2020, destroying 517 homes from Rainbow down to Vida. The McKenzie Community Land Trust has since opened six homes of about 1300 square feet on Rose Street, Blue River, priced $250K to $275K, with buyers owning the house and leasing the land on a 99-year land lease. Figures reported through September 2025, from KLCC and KVAL. Land-trust pricing and availability are that organization's, not ours, and change — confirm current terms with them directly.

What an inspector finds here

A rebuilding market inspects unlike anything else in the county. New construction on burned lots, bare land with existing utilities, surviving originals, and land-trust homes each need a completely different set of questions.

Who is buying

Rebuild buyers, land buyers, and people with a long connection to the upper McKenzie. Very few of them are shopping the rest of Lane County at the same time.

Selling here

Blue River doesn’t comp like a normal market. New construction, bare lots, surviving originals and land-trust homes each price on a different logic, so a single Blue River median tells a seller almost nothing.

When this market moves

Rebuild activity runs on construction season rather than on a buying season, which makes the calendar here look nothing like the valley's.

The market behind the number

Lane County's median sale price ran about $464,000 for the three months ending June 2026, down 3.5% from a year earlier, with a median of 18 days on market (Redfin). Rural Lane County transacts in much lower volume than the metro core, which makes county medians a poor proxy for any individual property. Valuation here comes from comparable sales plus documented adjustments for acreage, access, water and outbuildings.

About these figures. Market data is county- or city-level and carries its source and date. It’s not a valuation of any specific property, and it’s not a promise about what your house will sell for. Ask us for a comparative market analysis on your address.

Sources for the figures on this page: KLCC reporting on the Holiday Farm Fire and the Blue River rebuild · KVAL: six new homes mark a rebuilding milestone in Blue River.

What trips up Blue River sales

Wildfire risk scoring affects which carriers will write a policy, and a buyer who can’t get a binder can’t close.
Fire history and defensible-space documentation should be assembled before the property is marketed.
Well flow test and water quality results belong in the listing file before marketing begins.
Questions

Asked and answered

How fast do houses sell in Blue River?

County-wide the median was 18 days on market in June 2026, down from 22 a year earlier (Redfin). Blue River is a smaller market than the Eugene–Springfield core, so individual listings vary far more than the county median suggests. The honest answer for your address comes from a CMA, not from an average.

What is my Blue River house worth?

An automated estimate is a starting point and nothing more — it can’t see condition, view, access, or what the last three comparable sales actually closed at. We run a comparative market analysis on the address and, at the same time, price all four exits so you can see the spread.

Can I sell in Blue River without listing it publicly?

Yes. Exit 02 is an off-market sale to our curated list of more than 700 accredited and cash investors — no sign, no public listing, no showings. It typically nets less than a full RMLS listing, and we tell you how much less before you choose it.

Do you work outside Eugene and Springfield?

All of Lane County, and beyond it all of Oregon. Rural and unincorporated property is a large share of what we handle — wells, septic systems, acreage and county land use are normal parts of the job out here.

What is the first thing I should do if I am selling in Blue River?

Work out which of the four Blue River products you’re selling — new build, bare lot, survivor or land-trust home — because each one is priced on a different logic.

What does it cost to sell a house in Blue River?

Commission, title and escrow, any concessions you agree to, and the repairs you choose to make. On Blue River property the repair line is the variable one, which is why the inspection profile above matters to your net. Commissions are negotiable and are not set by law or by any association, and ours is paid at closing out of the proceeds — there’s nothing to pay up front. The number to compare between offers is net proceeds, not gross price.
