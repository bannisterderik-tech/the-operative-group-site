The four exits

Same house, four different numbers. Here’s what each one costs you and what it buys you.

The short version

A house can leave your hands four different ways, and each one produces a different number. Listing on the RMLS usually produces the highest gross price and takes the longest. Selling off market to an investor is faster and quieter and nets less. Selling to our own portfolio is the fastest and most certain and nets less still. An institutional offer sits in between and is useful as a benchmark even when you don’t take it.

The mistake is picking one before you have seen the other three. We run all four for your address and put the numbers next to each other. That takes about an hour and costs nothing.

How to choose

Start with the deadline. If there’s a hard date — a court timeline, a closing you’re chained to, a move-in at a senior community — certainty is worth more than the last few percent, and exits 02 and 03 get serious.

Then look at condition. A house that needs a roof, a foundation repair, or a full clean-out will lose financed buyers at the inspection. Exits 02 and 03 don’t care. Exit 01 cares a great deal.

Then look at privacy. A divorce, an estate, a tenant situation, a business partner who doesn’t know yet. Off market means no sign, no public listing, and no neighbors at an open house.

Then do the arithmetic. Gross price minus repairs, minus commission, minus concessions, minus the months of taxes and insurance and utilities you’ll pay while it sits. That’s the number to compare, and it’s often not the one people compare.

Questions

Asked and answered

Which exit nets the most money?

Usually the RMLS listing, if the house shows well and you have the time. The other three trade some of that premium for speed, privacy, or the ability to sell as-is. The right question isn’t which nets most, it’s which nets most after the repairs, the carrying cost and the risk you would actually take on.

Is it a conflict of interest if you buy my house?

Yes, and we disclose it in writing. When The Operative Group is the buyer we’re not acting as your agent in that transaction, and we tell you to get an independent opinion of value. That’s the only honest way to offer this option.

How big is the off-market buyer list?

More than 700 curated accredited and cash investor contacts, built over years of the partners buying and selling in this market themselves.

Can I see all four numbers before I commit to anything?

That’s the point of the exercise. We run the comparative market analysis, get the off-market indications, price the portfolio option and pull the institutional number, then you decide. No listing agreement is required to have that conversation.

What if the house needs a lot of work?

Then the off-market and portfolio exits usually win, because neither requires repairs, showings or a clean-out. Run it both ways anyway — sometimes a small repair spend unlocks a much bigger buyer pool.
