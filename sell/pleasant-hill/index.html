Sell a house in
Pleasant Hill

Highway 58 southeast of Eugene, unincorporated.

Buyers come out here for the district and the acreage, not for Highway 58 traffic, which means the marketing radius is Eugene, not the corridor. The listing has to reach people who aren’t driving past it.

What Pleasant Hill is

Highway 58 southeast of Eugene, unincorporated. Country properties on acreage, plus houses on smaller lots near the school campus.

  • Pleasant Hill School District, a draw for families out of Eugene
  • No city limits — Lane County zoning applies
  • Acreage parcels are common
  • Fifteen to twenty minutes to south Eugene

What an inspector finds here

Acreage properties on wells and septic, plus older houses near the school campus. Outbuildings, fencing and access roads all come into the conversation.

Who is buying

Buyers coming out of Eugene for the district and the acreage, financing conventionally, with a smaller group buying small farms.

Selling here

Market to Eugene, not to the highway. The buyer who pays the most for a Pleasant Hill property is already living in Eugene and isn’t driving past your sign, so the listing has to reach them where they already are.

When this market moves

School-district buyers want to be in before the school year, which concentrates activity into spring and early summer and thins it sharply after August.

The market behind the number

Lane County's median sale price ran about $464,000 for the three months ending June 2026, down 3.5% from a year earlier, with a median of 18 days on market (Redfin). Rural Lane County transacts in much lower volume than the metro core, which makes county medians a poor proxy for any individual property. Valuation here comes from comparable sales plus documented adjustments for acreage, access, water and outbuildings.

What trips up Pleasant Hill sales

Well flow test and water quality results belong in the listing file before marketing begins.
Septic records and a septic inspection are standard buyer requests outside city limits.
Legal access and deeded easements need to be confirmed, not assumed.
Questions

Asked and answered

How fast do houses sell in Pleasant Hill?

County-wide the median was 18 days on market in June 2026, down from 22 a year earlier (Redfin). Pleasant Hill is a smaller market than the Eugene–Springfield core, so individual listings vary far more than the county median suggests. The honest answer for your address comes from a CMA, not from an average.

What is my Pleasant Hill house worth?

An automated estimate is a starting point and nothing more — it can’t see condition, view, access, or what the last three comparable sales actually closed at. We run a comparative market analysis on the address and, at the same time, price all four exits so you can see the spread.

Can I sell in Pleasant Hill without listing it publicly?

Yes. Exit 02 is an off-market sale to our curated list of more than 700 accredited and cash investors — no sign, no public listing, no showings. It typically nets less than a full RMLS listing, and we tell you how much less before you choose it.

Do you work outside Eugene and Springfield?

All of Lane County, and beyond it all of Oregon. Rural and unincorporated property is a large share of what we handle — wells, septic systems, acreage and county land use are normal parts of the job out here.

What is the first thing I should do if I am selling in Pleasant Hill?

Confirm the school district boundary for the exact address in writing. It’s the single biggest draw and buyers will verify it.

What does it cost to sell a house in Pleasant Hill?

Commission, title and escrow, any concessions you agree to, and the repairs you choose to make. On Pleasant Hill property the repair line is the variable one, which is why the inspection profile above matters to your net. Commissions are negotiable and are not set by law or by any association, and ours is paid at closing out of the proceeds — there’s nothing to pay up front. The number to compare between offers is net proceeds, not gross price.
