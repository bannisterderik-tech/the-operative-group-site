Sell a house in
Noti, Crow & Cheshire

The west Lane County country along Highways 126 and 36.

Out here the land is often worth more than the house, and timber, water rights and zoning can each move the number. We value the parcel and the dwelling separately, because the buyer will.

What Noti, Crow & Cheshire is

The west Lane County country along Highways 126 and 36. Farms, timber parcels, manufactured homes on land, and rural houses.

  • Crow-Applegate-Lorane School District serves much of the area
  • Unincorporated Lane County throughout
  • Timber and farm ground dominate the land use
  • Wells and septic systems are the norm

What an inspector finds here

Farms, timber parcels and rural houses: well and septic, legal and practical access, and the condition of outbuildings that often carry real value.

Who is buying

Land buyers, timber buyers, and people looking for acreage within reach of Eugene. Financing is a mix of conventional, cash and farm credit.

Selling here

Price the land and the dwelling separately, and document both. A buyer out here’s underwriting acreage, timber, water and access alongside the house, and an appraiser will want the same separation you should have started with.

When this market moves

Land and timber sell on their own clock, driven more by commodity prices and by what the buyer needs to do with the parcel than by the month.

Compare all four exits

The market behind the number

Lane County's median sale price ran about $464,000 for the three months ending June 2026, down 3.5% from a year earlier, with a median of 18 days on market (Redfin). Rural Lane County transacts in much lower volume than the metro core, which makes county medians a poor proxy for any individual property. Valuation here comes from comparable sales plus documented adjustments for acreage, access, water and outbuildings.

About these figures. Market data is county- or city-level and carries its source and date. It’s not a valuation of any specific property, and it’s not a promise about what your house will sell for. Ask us for a comparative market analysis on your address.

Watch for

What trips up Noti, Crow & Cheshire sales

Well flow test and water quality results belong in the listing file before marketing begins.
Septic records and a septic inspection are standard buyer requests outside city limits.
Legal access and deeded easements need to be confirmed, not assumed.
Questions

Asked and answered

How fast do houses sell in Noti, Crow & Cheshire?

County-wide the median was 18 days on market in June 2026, down from 22 a year earlier (Redfin). Noti, Crow & Cheshire is a smaller market than the Eugene–Springfield core, so individual listings vary far more than the county median suggests. The honest answer for your address comes from a CMA, not from an average.

What is my Noti, Crow & Cheshire house worth?

An automated estimate is a starting point and nothing more — it can’t see condition, view, access, or what the last three comparable sales actually closed at. We run a comparative market analysis on the address and, at the same time, price all four exits so you can see the spread.

Can I sell in Noti, Crow & Cheshire without listing it publicly?

Yes. Exit 02 is an off-market sale to our curated list of more than 700 accredited and cash investors — no sign, no public listing, no showings. It typically nets less than a full RMLS listing, and we tell you how much less before you choose it.

Do you work outside Eugene and Springfield?

All of Lane County, and beyond it all of Oregon. Rural and unincorporated property is a large share of what we handle — wells, septic systems, acreage and county land use are normal parts of the job out here.

What is the first thing I should do if I am selling in Noti, Crow & Cheshire?

Get the zoning, the water rights and the legal access confirmed in writing. Out here those three documents are most of what a buyer is purchasing.

What does it cost to sell a house in Noti, Crow & Cheshire?

Commission, title and escrow, any concessions you agree to, and the repairs you choose to make. On Noti, Crow & Cheshire property the repair line is the variable one, which is why the inspection profile above matters to your net. Commissions are negotiable and are not set by law or by any association, and ours is paid at closing out of the proceeds — there’s nothing to pay up front. The number to compare between offers is net proceeds, not gross price.
