How long do creditors have to make a claim against an Oregon estate?

Short answer

A claim is barred unless presented before the later of two dates: four months after the first publication of notice to interested persons, or 30 days after a required direct notice is delivered or mailed to that claimant. That is ORS 115.005.

ORS 115.005

Checked September 2026

The two-part test matters. Publishing notice starts a four-month clock for the world, but a creditor you knew about and notified directly gets at least 30 days from that notice, even if the four months has nearly run.

This is why the order of operations in an estate is not arbitrary. Publishing early starts the clock early.

For a sale, the claim window is what a title company is really watching. It wants to know that claims are handled or barred before it insures a transfer to a new owner.
