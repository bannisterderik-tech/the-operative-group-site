Oregon probate, county by county

Oregon probate is filed in the circuit court for a county. Which county depends on where the person lived and where the property sits.

Short answer

Oregon probate is filed in the circuit court for a county, and which county depends on where the decedent lived and where the property sits. Your attorney confirms the venue before anything is filed. Oregon has 36 county circuit courts grouped into 27 judicial districts. Estates under $275,000 may qualify for a Small Estate Affidavit instead of a full administration.

ORS 113.015 venue · ORS 114.325

Checked September 2026

The procedure’s the same across Oregon — the same thresholds, the same creditor window, the same authority to sell. What changes is the courthouse. Pick the county the person lived in.

Questions

Asked and answered

Where do I file probate in Oregon?

In the circuit court for the county where the decedent lived, with the location of the property also relevant. Oregon runs 36 county circuit courts inside 27 judicial districts, and your attorney settles venue before the petition goes in.

What is the small estate limit in Oregon?

$275,000 in total: up to $200,000 of real property and up to $75,000 of personal property. The affidavit can be filed 30 days after the death, for a $124 fee.

How many circuit courts does Oregon have?

One per county, so 36, grouped into 27 judicial districts. Six districts cover more than one county; the rest cover a single county.
