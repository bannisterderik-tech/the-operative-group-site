Probate in Lane County

Filed at the Lane County Circuit Court in Eugene, in Oregon’s 2nd judicial district.

Short answer

An estate for someone who lived in Lane County is filed in the Lane County Circuit Court at Eugene, in Oregon’s 2nd judicial district. If the estate is under $275,000 total — no more than $200,000 of real property and $75,000 of personal property — a Small Estate Affidavit filed after 30 days may be enough, with no full administration at all.

ORS 114.325 · ORS 115.005

Checked September 2026

Where it gets filed

Lane County has the 2nd judicial district to itself. There’s one circuit court, at Eugene, and that’s where the petition goes.

The court at Eugene covers estates across the whole county, including Eugene, Springfield, Cottage Grove, Florence, Junction City and Creswell and 6 other incorporated cities, together with all the unincorporated land between them.

About 74% of Lane County lives inside a city limit; roughly 98,072 residents don’t. Estate property out there usually carries a well, a septic system and an access question, and all three want answering in the title report first.

This is the home court. The Lane County Circuit Court at 125 East 8th Avenue, Eugene, OR 97401 is where this team has run estate sales, and the Probate Department, 2nd floor is where the file lives.

Selling the house out of the estate

Under ORS 114.325 the personal representative may generally sell or convey the real property without a court hearing, so a Lane County sale usually isn’t waiting on a court date. The full sequence, and the path finder to tell you in a minute whether this is a small estate or a full administration.

Questions

Asked and answered

Which court handles probate in Lane County, Oregon?

The Lane County Circuit Court, which sits at Eugene in Oregon’s 2nd judicial district.

Can a personal representative sell a Lane County house without a court hearing?

Usually yes. Under ORS 114.325, unless the will says otherwise, the personal representative may sell or convey real property without a court hearing, with stated exceptions. Confirm your own situation with your attorney.

Does a small estate affidavit work in Lane County?

Yes, if the estate fits the statewide ceiling — see the thresholds table above. Filed at Eugene.
