Net operating income

Short answer

A property’s income after vacancy and operating expenses, but before debt service, depreciation and income tax.

Checked September 2026

What it means in Oregon

Because it excludes financing, NOI describes the building rather than the owner, which is what makes it comparable across deals.

Every income valuation method starts here, so an inflated NOI inflates everything downstream.

Related terms

  • Capitalisation rate — Net operating income divided by price, expressed as a percentage. It compares income properties to each other independently of how they are financed.
  • APOD — Annual Property Operating Data: a one-page statement running a rental property’s gross income down through vacancy and operating expenses to net operating income.
