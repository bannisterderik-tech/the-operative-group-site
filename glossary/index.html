Oregon real estate glossary

30 terms, each defined in a sentence and then explained the way it actually works in Oregon. Statutes, thresholds and county practice included.

Most glossaries define a term and stop. The definition is rarely the hard part — what people actually need is what the term does in the state they are buying in. A rent cap means one thing in general and a specific number in Oregon; an ADU is a concept nationally and a set of square-footage limits in Eugene.
