What happens on the call

We listen first.
That’s step one for a reason.

The first conversation is a listening appointment. We ask about the situation, the timeline, and what a good outcome looks like for you — before anyone talks about price, commission or a listing agreement.

If you’re selling, you leave with a comparative market analysis on your address and an honest read on which of the four exits fits. If you’re buying, you leave knowing what your financing supports and where in Lane County that money goes furthest. If the estate needs an attorney more than it needs an agent, we say that and refer you to one.

Serving all of Lane County — Eugene, Springfield, Cottage Grove, Creswell, Junction City, Veneta, Florence, Oakridge, the McKenzie corridor and the Coast Range. Every advisor on this team is licensed in the State of Oregon under REAL Broker.

Nothing discussed on a first call creates an agency relationship — that happens in writing, when you decide it should.
