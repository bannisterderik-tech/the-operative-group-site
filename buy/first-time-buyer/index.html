Questions

Asked and answered

What do I actually need for a down payment in Lane County?

It depends on the loan. FHA runs 3.5% down, conventional programs go as low as 3%, and Oregon has down-payment assistance programs a good lender will walk you through. On a $400,000 Lane County house that’s roughly $12,000 to $14,000 down, plus closing costs — which are often negotiable as a seller concession.

How many offers should I expect to write?

Plan for more than one and be pleasantly surprised. The median Lane County house went pending in 18 days in June 2026, so speed and clean terms matter as much as price. We tell you before you write what the listing agent is likely to prioritize.

What repairs will kill my loan?

FHA appraisals apply minimum property standards — peeling paint on a pre-1978 house, missing handrails, an inoperable furnace, a roof at end of life. We screen for those before you write, because discovering them at the appraisal costs you two weeks and sometimes the house.
