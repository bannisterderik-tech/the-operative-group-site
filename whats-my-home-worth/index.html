The honest answer is that no website knows. Here’s what determines the number, and how we work it out for your address.

What an online estimate can and can’t see

Automated valuations are built from tax records and closed sales. They’re a reasonable starting point on a 1998 tract house on a flat lot in a subdivision where twenty near-identical homes sold last year. They’re close to meaningless on a river parcel, a hillside lot, a house with an unpermitted addition, or anything on a well and septic.

What sets the number

  • Condition, honestly assessed — roof, systems, foundation, and what an inspector will write
  • What genuinely comparable properties closed at, with adjustments you can defend to an appraiser
  • What is competing with you right now, and what has already failed to sell
  • Which buyer pool you’re selling to, and how that pool is financed
  • For rural property: water, septic, access, acreage and outbuildings, valued separately from the dwelling

And then the part nobody else runs

Gross price isn’t what you keep. We price all four exits — a prepared RMLS listing, an off-market sale to our 700+ investor list, a direct purchase into the partners' portfolio, and an institutional offer — and put the net proceeds side by side. That’s the number to make a decision on.

Questions

Asked and answered

Why is the online estimate wrong on my house?

Automated valuations work from public records and past sales. They can’t see condition, a remodel, a view, a failing roof, a shared driveway, a septic system, or the fact that the comparable two doors down had been gutted. On rural Lane County property, where parcels differ enormously, the miss is often six figures.

What is in a comparative market analysis?

Recent comparable sales with adjustments, current competing listings, what has failed to sell and why, days on market for your price band, and the condition and preparation gap between your house and the ones setting the price. Plus, from us, what each of the four exits would net after costs.

Does it cost anything?

No, and it doesn’t require a listing agreement. We would rather you have a real number and decide not to sell than list on a guess.

How long does it take?

About an hour on site, plus the time to pull the off-market and institutional indications if you want all four numbers.
